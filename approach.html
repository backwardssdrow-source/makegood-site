Our approach

Listen carefully.
Make it practical.

We start with how people experience the organization, then examine the roles, relationships, resources, and decisions shaping that experience.

The aim is to improve how people work together, not simply ask individuals to adapt to the same conditions.

Why this work matters now

As work changes,
keep people connected.

When roles, priorities, and ways of working shift, it can become harder for people to know where they have a voice or how to contribute. Employee communities need clear ways to bring those experiences into organizational decisions.

Your ERGs may have outgrown informal ways of working. Leaders may have responsibility without clear authority or sponsor support. We help make those needs visible and turn them into practical changes.

01 / Listen

Understand the problem.

Listen to people’s experiences and look at how the work is organized.

How this works

We connect what people tell us with who can make decisions, what support exists, and where responsibilities are unclear. Interviews and document review are included when agreed for the service.

For example: people aren’t coming back.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study.

An employee program attracts initial interest, but people rarely return. Rather than assume it needs more promotion, we look at whose interests shaped it, what makes participation difficult, and whether people have a say in what happens next.

Depending on what we learn, the next step might be a different format, more accessible timing, or a clearer way for participants to shape the program.

02 / Make sense

Choose what to change.

Find shared concerns and goals, while making differences and trade-offs clear.

How this works

We bring concerns together and look for shared goals, even when they first seem unrelated. Community advocacy and organizational priorities both need to be understood before choosing a response.

03 / Build

Put the plan to work.

Agree on practical changes, responsibilities, and follow-through.

How this works

We develop next steps with the people involved and consider those affected by the decisions. Depending on the service, this may mean a recommendation, a leadership practice, a program guide, or an implementation plan.

Before we start

We agree on confidentiality, the time people can give, what is included, and who owns the next steps.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what is happening. We’ll help you identify the right kind of support.
